# Giggle Sprouts branding design

## Scope

Apply the supplied Giggle Sprouts logo to the existing SproutOps dashboard header.

## Header design

- Add the supplied `logo_new.png` to a project-owned asset location under `app/`.
- Replace the current text-only heading with a horizontal brand row: the logo on the left and the existing `SproutOps` title on the right.
- Keep the logo proportional and provide useful alternative text.
- Place the subheading on its own line below the brand row, centered within the header.
- Use the exact subheading: `Operations center for Giggle Sprouts`.

## Responsive behavior

- Preserve the horizontal logo-and-title relationship on narrow screens while reducing the logo and title size as needed.
- Keep the centered subheading readable without overflow or clipping.

## Validation

- Confirm that the page is served successfully from the existing local static server.
- Inspect the header at desktop and mobile-width viewports to confirm the logo remains visible, undistorted, and the subheading remains centered.
